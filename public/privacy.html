1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a DECENT account, we collect:

  • Email address and password (stored securely and hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password)
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by Google
  • Your chosen display name, handle/username, profile photo, role/title, location, and bio, if you provide them
  • Social media links you choose to add to your profile

Portfolio Content

Anything you upload or type when creating a portfolio, including:

  • Titles, briefs, and long-form descriptions
  • Cover images and showcase images
  • Figma prototype links and embedded files
  • Video demo links
  • Category/genre tags you select
  • Your NSFW designation and AI-generated content disclosure for that portfolio

Activity & Engagement Data

  • Portfolios you like, designers you follow, and portfolios you view
  • Comments or feedback you submit through in-app feedback forms
  • Reports you file against other content or users, including any details you provide
  • Users you block or mute

Device & Notification Data

  • A push notification token, if you enable notifications, so we can deliver them to your device
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app (device platform, app version)

Information Stored Only On Your Device

Some preferences never leave your device and are not sent to our servers. These include your light/dark theme choice, Fancy Mode setting, For You feed filters, Safe Search preference, and which in-app tutorials you've seen. Clearing your app data or reinstalling the app resets these.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and maintain your account and authenticate you when you sign in
  • Display your profile and portfolios to other users, as intended by the app's core purpose
  • Power features like following, liking, notifications, and search
  • Review and act on content reports, for community safety and moderation
  • Send you notifications you've opted into (e.g., new followers, likes)
  • Understand which upcoming features people want, so we can prioritize development (see Section 4)
  • Diagnose and fix bugs when the app encounters an error

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your content to train third-party advertising profiles.

3. AI Content Disclosure

When uploading a portfolio, you're asked to disclose whether AI played any role in creating that work. This disclosure is self-reported by the uploader — we do not independently verify it through automated detection. We rely on accurate reporting from our community, and on user reports (see Section 1) to flag content that appears mislabeled. Portfolios found to knowingly misrepresent AI involvement may be relabeled by moderators and, in repeated or severe cases, may result in account action.

4. Feature Interest Tracking

When you tap "I'm Interested" on an upcoming feature (such as Frontend Development or Graphic Design portfolios), we record that your account expressed interest in that specific feature. This is tied to your account, not anonymous, and is used solely to gauge real demand before we build a feature. We may occasionally reach out to people who expressed interest with a short survey about what they'd want from that feature. You can remove your interest at any time from within the app.

6. Your Choices & Controls

  • Access and export: You can request an export of your account data from within the app's settings.
  • Correction: You can edit your profile and portfolio information at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual portfolios, or your entire account, from within the app. Deleting your account removes your profile and portfolios from public view; see Section 7 for how long backups may persist.
  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings or in-app.
  • Safe Search & content filters: You can control whether NSFW content or AI-generated content appears in your feed from Privacy settings.
  • Blocking: You can block or mute any user to stop seeing their content and prevent them from interacting with yours.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and portfolio data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your public-facing data promptly; residual copies may persist in backups for a limited period before being purged. Reports and moderation records may be retained longer where needed for safety and legal purposes.

8. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including encrypted password storage (via bcrypt hashing) and access-controlled databases (via row-level security policies restricting who can read or write which data). No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

9. Children's Privacy

DECENT is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll note it in the app's "What's New" changelog. Continued use of DECENT after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
